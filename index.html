01 / The opportunity

The infrastructure is already there. The opportunity is new.

The Pavilion's multi-level atrium offers a powerful setting for a flagship transparent LED escalator advertising installation.

Elevate Network proposes integrating transparent LED displays with suitable escalator glass balustrades, creating a distinctive format for high-impact animated brand storytelling without introducing freestanding structures into the retail floor.

This Pavilion installation would be the first step toward a carefully managed network across selected Pareto properties, subject to engineering review, approvals, financing and commercial agreement.

06 / A proposed strategic advantage

24 months. One property partner.

Elevate is prepared to offer Pareto a 24-month national exclusivity arrangement for shopping-centre escalator LED advertising installations.

During the agreed exclusivity period, Elevate would not approach competing shopping-centre ownership or management groups for equivalent escalator LED deployments. The focus would be on a controlled Pavilion-first rollout into mutually selected Pareto properties.

Exclusivity is offered for discussion, subject to mutually agreed activation milestones and commercial commitments. Elevate retains its intellectual property and its ability to pursue other technology applications outside the defined scope.
